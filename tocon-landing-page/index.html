A PROFESSIONAL APPROACH WITH A PERSONAL TOUCH

My name is Nico, and I work as an electrician, air-conditioning installer, and renovation specialist in the Roermond region.What sets me apart is the way I look at each project: not just the task in front of me, but everything around it.

Sometimes customers hire me for one specific job — replacing a consumer unit, installing an air conditioner, or placing a charging station.But often, I'm asked to take on a bigger role: thinking along, advising where needed (and yes, sometimes even unasked), and keeping an eye on the entire project.

Over the years, I've noticed that many problems arise because nobody looks ahead.A bathroom renovation affects the electrical layout. New windows affect ventilation.An air-conditioning system affects energy consumption and insulation.

I help customers avoid unpleasant surprises later by thinking a few steps ahead and making sure every detail fits into the bigger plan.
